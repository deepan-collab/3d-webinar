Frequently Asked Questions

What is your typical turnaround time?

▼
Most projects are delivered within 24-48 hours of approval. Complex designs may take 3-5 business days. Rush delivery available on request.

Do you provide unlimited revisions?

▼
Yes! We work until you're 100% satisfied. We believe in quality and client happiness. Revisions are included in all projects.

What file formats will I receive?

▼
High-resolution print files (CMYK 300DPI), web-optimized formats (JPG, PNG), and vector source files (AI, PDF). All include layered PSD files.

Can you match my existing brand colors?

▼
Absolutely. Share your brand guidelines or Pantone codes. We maintain 100% color accuracy across all mediums and formats.

What's included in the ₹1 consultation?

▼
A 30-minute session where we understand your project, provide technical direction, suggest design approaches, and give a free custom quote with no obligation.

How do I start working with you?

▼
Fill out the form below or WhatsApp us. We'll evaluate your project, provide options and pricing, and set up a call if needed. Simple and transparent.

Do you work with international clients?

▼
Yes! We work globally. Time zone is no issue—we respond within 8 hours. We accept all major payment methods for international orders.

What payment methods do you accept?

▼
We accept all major payment methods: UPI, Credit/Debit Cards, Bank Transfer, and Digital Wallets. 50% upfront, 50% on final delivery.
